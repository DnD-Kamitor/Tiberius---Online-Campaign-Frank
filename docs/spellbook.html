Casting TimeAction
Range30 ft
ComponentsV

You manifest a minor wonder, a sign of supernatural power. Choose one of the following effects:

• Your voice booms up to 3× louder for 1 minute.

• You cause harmless tremors in the ground for 1 minute.
• You create an instantaneous sound that originates from a point you choose within range.

Command Enchantment · 1st Level · Cleric (Acolyte)
Casting TimeAction
Range60 ft
ComponentsV
Duration1 round

You speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a WIS saving throw (DC 15) or follow the command on its next turn.

The spell has no effect if the target is Undead, if it doesn't understand your language, or if your command is directly harmful to it.

Example commands: Approach (move toward you), Drop (drops held items), Flee (move away from you), Grovel (falls prone and ends turn), Halt (doesn't move, no actions/reactions).

Higher Slots: Target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

Ritual
Casting TimeAction / Ritual +10 min
Range60 ft
ComponentsV, S, M (string)
Duration1 hour

This spell creates an invisible, mindless, shapeless force that performs simple tasks at your command until the spell ends. It has STR 2, AC 10, and 1 Hit Point. It disappears if reduced to 0 HP.

Find Familiar Conjuration · 1st Level · Wizard · Ritual
Ritual
Casting Time1 hour (ritual)
Range10 ft
ComponentsV, S, M (10 gp consumed)
DurationUntil dismissed

You gain the service of a familiar, a spirit that takes an animal form of your choice: bat, cat, crab, frog, hawk, lizard, octopus, owl, rat, raven, sea horse, spider, or weasel.

As an Action, you can see through the familiar's eyes and hear through its ears until the start of your next turn. You can communicate telepathically with it within 100 ft.

When you cast a spell with a touch range, your familiar can deliver it as if it cast the spell, as long as it is within 100 ft of you.

If the familiar drops to 0 HP, it disappears. You can resummon it by recasting the spell (consumes 10 gp again).

Comprehend Languages Divination · 1st Level · Wizard · Ritual
Ritual
Casting TimeAction / Ritual +10 min
RangeSelf
ComponentsV, S, M (soot, salt)
Duration1 hour

For the duration, you understand the literal meaning of any spoken language that you hear. You also understand any written language that you see, but you must be touching the surface on which the words are written. It takes about 1 minute to read one page of text.

This spell doesn't decode secret messages or glyphs that aren't part of a written language.

Does not grant the ability to speak or write the language.

Tenser's Floating Disk Conjuration · 1st Level · Wizard · Ritual
Ritual
Casting TimeAction / Ritual +10 min
Range30 ft
ComponentsV, S, M (mercury drop)
Duration1 hour

This spell creates a circular, horizontal plane of force, 3 feet in diameter and 1 inch thick, that floats 3 feet above the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. The disk persists for the duration and can hold up to 500 pounds.

If more weight is placed on it, the spell ends. The disk remains level even on inclined surfaces.

If you move more than 20 feet away from it, the disk follows you. If you are ever more than 100 feet away from it, the spell ends.

Silent Image Illusion · 1st Level · Wizard (costs 2nd-level slot)
C
Casting TimeAction
Range120 ft (60+60 Illusionist)
ComponentsS, M (fleece) — no verbal (Illusionist)
DurationConcentration, 10 min

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 15-foot cube. The image appears at a spot within range and lasts for the duration. The image is purely visual — it has no sound, smell, or other sensory effects.

As an Action, you can cause the image to move to any spot within range. As the image changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural for the image.

Physical interaction reveals the image as an illusion. A creature that uses its action to examine the image can determine it is illusory with a successful Investigation check (DC 15).

Silvery Barbs Enchantment · 1st Level · Wizard (costs 2nd-level slot)
Reaction
Casting TimeReaction*
Range60 ft
ComponentsV
DurationInstantaneous

*Trigger: a creature within 60 ft succeeds on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

You unleash a barrage of needle-like shards of silver that force the triggering creature to reroll the d20 and use the lower roll, potentially turning the success into a failure.

You can also choose a different creature you can see within range (not the triggering creature). That creature gains advantage on the next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw it makes within 1 minute. A creature can have this benefit only once per casting.

One of the strongest reaction spells in the game — can deny a critical hit or a passed saving throw.

Color Spray ⭐ Illusion · 1st Level · Wizard · Prepared
Prepared
Casting TimeAction
RangeSelf (15-ft cone)
ComponentsS, M (sand/powder) — no verbal (Illusionist)
Duration1 round

A dazzling array of flashing, coloured light springs from your hand. Roll 6d10; the total is how many Hit Points of creatures this spell can affect. Starting with the creature that has the lowest current HP, each creature affected by this spell is Blinded until the end of your next turn.

Creatures in the area are affected in ascending order of their current Hit Points. Subtract each creature's Hit Points from the total before moving to the next. A creature's HP must be equal to or less than the remaining total for the creature to be affected.

Note: Can be cast at 1st or 2nd slot too — just affects fewer HP worth of creatures (no 3rd slot yet).

Higher Slots: Roll an additional 2d10 for each slot level above 1st.

Magic Weapon Transmutation · 2nd Level · Wizard
C
Casting TimeBonus Action
RangeTouch
ComponentsV, S
DurationConcentration, 1 hour

You touch a nonmagical weapon. Until the spell ends, that weapon becomes a magic weapon with a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls.

This means it bypasses resistance and immunity to damage from nonmagical attacks — crucial against constructs, undead, and similar creatures.

Great on your Warhammer or Light Crossbow before a fight you know involves resistant creatures.

Higher Slots: +2 bonus with a 4th-level slot · +3 bonus with a 6th-level slot

Nystul's Magic Aura Illusion · 2nd Level · Wizard (Illusionist)
Casting TimeAction
RangeTouch
ComponentsS, M (silk square) — no verbal (Illusionist)
Duration24 hours

You place an illusion on a creature or object you touch so that divination spells reveal false information about it. The target can be a willing creature or an object not carried or worn by another creature.

Choose one or both of the following effects when you cast the spell:

False Aura: You change the way the target appears to spells and magical effects that detect magical auras, such as Detect Magic. You can make a nonmagical object appear magical, a magical object appear nonmagical, or change the object's magical aura to appear as a different school of magic you choose.

Mask: You change the way the target appears to spells and magical effects that detect creature types, such as a paladin's Divine Sense. You choose a creature type and other spells treat the target as if it were that type.

If you cast this spell on the same creature or object every day for 30 days, placing the same effect on it each time, the illusion lasts until it is dispelled.

Rope Trick Transmutation · 2nd Level · Wizard
Casting TimeAction
RangeTouch
ComponentsV, S, M (powdered corn)
Duration1 hour

You touch a length of rope that is up to 60 feet long. One end of the rope then rises into the air until the whole rope hangs perpendicular to the ground. At the upper end of the rope, an invisible entrance opens to an extradimensional space that lasts until the spell ends.

The space can hold as many as eight Medium or smaller creatures. Attacks and spells cannot pass through the entrance into or out of the extradimensional space, but those inside can see through the entrance as if looking through a 3-foot-by-5-foot window centered on the rope.

Anything inside the space drops out when the spell ends. The rope can be picked up or moved, and if pulled into the space, it stays there. The entrance is invisible from outside.
