Javelin of Lightning

Weapon · Uncommon · No attunement required

Acquired Sess. 0 · 2026-05-22 · Cost: 600 GP

When you throw this javelin and speak its command word, it transforms into a bolt of lightning, forming a line 5 feet wide and 120 feet long. Each creature in the line (excluding you) must succeed on a DC 13 DEX saving throw or take 4d6 lightning damage (half on success). The javelin then returns to its normal form at the target point. Can’t be used this way again until the next dawn.
